# 🚀 AI-Powered Customer Retention Intelligence System

An end-to-end **Customer Churn Prediction and Business Intelligence** solution that combines **Machine Learning** and **Power BI** to identify high-risk customers, estimate business impact, and support customer retention strategies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by subscription-based businesses. This project predicts customer churn using machine learning and transforms the predictions into actionable business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The solution includes:

- Customer Churn Prediction
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Estimation
- Revenue at Risk Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Priority Score Calculation
- Retention Recommendations
- Executive Business Dashboard

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Predict customers likely to churn.
- Identify high-value customers requiring immediate attention.
- Estimate potential revenue loss due to churn.
- Segment customers based on business value.
- Build an executive dashboard for business stakeholders.

---

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** IBM Telco Customer Churn

- Customers: **7,043**
- Features: **20+**
- Target Variable: **Churn**
